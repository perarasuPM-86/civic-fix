How CivicFix Works

The Report → Route → Track → Resolve Cycle

A closed-loop civic tech pipeline connecting proactive citizens with municipal public works crews.

01

Report

Citizens snap a photo, pick an issue category, drop a location pin, and submit a complaint in under 60 seconds.

02

Route

Smart categorization routes the ticket to the responsible department (Road Maintenance, Water, Sanitation) with priority ratings.

03

Track

Transparent 4-stage tracking timeline gives citizens full visibility from submission to field crew dispatch.

04

Resolve

Field teams execute remediation, supervisory officers verify quality, and the issue is officially signed off as resolved.
